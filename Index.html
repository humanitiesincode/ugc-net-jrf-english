Q08Standard MCQ

Why? Fuller shared Wollstonecraft's emphasis on education but went further in questioning fixed gender roles. She argued that no person is completely masculine or feminine, anticipating later theories of androgyny.
Q09Standard MCQ

Why? Woolf argues that aesthetics, literary merits and traditions established by male authors and critics are promoted as universal despite being male-centered. Women readers are then trained to accept these standards.
